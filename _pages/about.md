I am a Walmart Predoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Computer Science and Automation](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/) at the Indian Institute of Science, advised by [Sumit K. Mandal](https://sumitmandal.github.io/). I work on quantum computer systems, especially compilers, runtimes, and architectures for scalable and fault-tolerant quantum computing.

My work so far has spanned noise-aware resource allocation, compiler-pass attribution, quantum resource estimation, and topology-aware compilation. Across these projects, I have been interested in how information that is usually hidden behind an abstraction—program structure, hardware topology, or error-correction structure—can be exposed to make better systems decisions. This has led me toward fault-tolerant compilation, QEC-aware mapping and scheduling, resource-aware runtimes, and compiler–architecture co-design.

I am currently working with [Prakash Murali](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/people/pm830) at the University of Cambridge on hardware-efficient realizations of Bivariate Bicycle and Gross-family qLDPC codes, where we exploit code-graph structure to reduce connectivity while controlling syndrome-extraction overheads. I also spent Summer 2026 at Fujitsu Research India, hosted by [Aritra Sarkar](https://aritrasarkar.com/), working on quantum compilation and resource estimation.
