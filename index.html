I am a Walmart Predoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian Institute of Science, advised by Prof. Sumit K. Mandal. I work on quantum computer systems, especially compilers, runtimes, and architectures for scalable and fault-tolerant quantum computing.

My research asks what information about a fault-tolerant computation should remain visible to the software stack. Rather than treating error correction, logical execution, scheduling, and hardware constraints as separate layers, I study how their structure can be exposed to the compiler and runtime to reduce resource cost and improve reliability. I am particularly interested in fault-tolerant compilation, QEC-aware mapping and scheduling, resource-aware runtimes, and compiler–architecture co-design.

I am currently working with Prof. Prakash Murali at the University of Cambridge on hardware-efficient realizations of Bivariate Bicycle and Gross-family qLDPC codes, where we exploit code-graph structure to reduce connectivity while controlling syndrome-extraction overheads. I also spent Summer 2026 at Fujitsu Research India, hosted by Dr. Aritra Sarkar, working on quantum compilation and resource estimation.
