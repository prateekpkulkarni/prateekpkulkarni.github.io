About

I am a Walmart Predoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science, advised by Prof. Sumit K. Mandal. I work on quantum computer systems, especially compilers, runtimes, resource management, and hardware–software co-design for scalable and fault-tolerant quantum computing.

I will be a visiting researcher at the Fields Institute in Fall 2026.

Research

My current interests include quantum compilation and runtime systems, fault-tolerant quantum computing, QEC-aware mapping, resource allocation, and architecture–compiler co-design.
